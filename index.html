You're welcome here

We are a family of believers who gather to worship, to hear the undiluted Word of God and to see it at work in everyday lives.

Our vision is to raise an indigenous and self-supporting church, and to demonstrate the love of God in our generation. Whether you have walked with Jesus for years or are only beginning to ask questions, there is a seat for you.

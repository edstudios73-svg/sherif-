A house where faith is shown, not only spoken

Faith Demonstration Ministries International is a prophetic and apostolic ministry in Accra, Ghana. We gather to worship, to hear the Word of God and to see it at work in everyday lives: in healing, in restoration and in families made whole.

Whether you have walked with Jesus for years or you are only beginning to ask questions, you are welcome here.

Apostle Victor Annan

Leads the ministry

A minister of the Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ, Apostle Annan leads the ministry with a deep prophetic and apostolic grace.

Rev. Mrs Natalie Annan

Ministers alongside the Apostle

Rev. Mrs Annan serves alongside the Apostle, caring for the church family and ministering in worship and the Word.

Faith Demonstration Ministries International

A prophetic and apostolic church in Adjen-Kotoku, Accra

Led by Apostle Victor Annan, we gather to worship, study His Word and see the power of God at work. Our Divine Service is every Sunday from 8:30 AM. Come as you are.

What we're about

Faith that is shown, not only spoken

Everything we do comes from the Word of God and is led by the Holy Spirit.

Worship

Spirit-filled praise every service, and all-night worship on special nights.

The Word

Practical Bible teaching every Tuesday at Study His Word.

Prayer

Grace Hour on Wednesday mornings, open to everyone who needs prayer.

Liberation
